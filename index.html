    How to Play

    1. Enter a whole number between 1 and 100.
    2. Use the feedback to narrow down your next guess.
    3. Find the secret number before your guesses run out.
    4. A new round begins after you win or lose.

    Invalid entries do not reduce your remaining guesses. However, repeated valid guesses still count.
